FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the QR Bell device work?

You receive a physical QR code sticker or device. Place it anywhere — on your door, vehicle, or a stand. When a customer scans it, they see your profile and can contact you instantly.

Do I need a smartphone or special equipment?

Just a smartphone to receive lead notifications. The customer only needs a camera app to scan your QR code — no app download required.

How quickly do I get paid?

Earnings from the Pro and Business plans include weekly automatic payouts to your bank account or digital wallet.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel your subscription anytime from your dashboard settings.
